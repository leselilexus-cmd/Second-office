02 | Opportunities Identified

01

No professional website

Customers may not have one reliable place to understand the business, services, location, pricing or contact options.

02

No centralized customer journey

Information is spread across social platforms, making it harder for customers to move from discovery to enquiry or booking.

03

Social media is not fully conversion-focused

Content may create awareness, but it should guide customers toward a clear next action such as WhatsApp, booking or enquiry.

04

Google presence can be stronger

A stronger Google Business presence can make the business easier to find, trust and contact locally.

05

Brand presentation can be more consistent

Visual identity, messaging and calls-to-action should feel consistent across every customer touchpoint.

What this means for the client

The goal is not simply to build a website. The goal is to create a connected digital presence that helps the right customers discover the business, trust it, and take action.

03 | Second Office Recommendation

WEBSITE + GOOGLE PRESENCE + SOCIAL MEDIA OPTIMIZATION + BOOKING / WHATSAPP INTEGRATION

Recommended focus:

  • Professional website: services, about, location, contact, FAQs and clear calls-to-action.
  • Google presence: accurate business details, photos, reviews and customer contact information.
  • Social media optimization: consistent branding, profile structure, content direction and conversion-focused calls-to-action.
  • Booking / WhatsApp integration: make enquiries, reservations or service requests easy to complete.
