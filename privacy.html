Second Office ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or use our services.

We comply with the Data Protection Act, 2012 of Lesotho and other applicable laws.

By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

3. Information We Collect

When you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Your name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Company name and business details.
  • Any information you provide through contact forms, email, or WhatsApp.
  • Content you submit for projects (logos, photos, text, etc.).

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may collect:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Operating system.
  • Pages visited and time spent.
  • Referring website or link.
  • Device type.

3.3 Information from Third Parties

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Deliver the services you have hired us for.
  • Process payments and send invoices.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Send marketing communications (only with your consent).
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We will never sell your personal information to third parties.

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure our website functions properly.
  • Understand how users interact with our site.
  • Improve user experience.

5.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functions.
  • Analytics Cookies: To track page views and traffic sources (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Preference Cookies: To remember your preferences.

5.2 Your Choice

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. But note that some features may not work properly if you disable cookies.

These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and can only use your data to perform services on our behalf.

8. Data Security

We take the security of your information seriously.

8.1 Our Security Measures

  • Encryption of data in transit.
  • Access controls to limit who can see your information.
  • Regular security reviews.
  • Secure hosting environments.

8.2 Your Responsibility

  • No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
  • You are responsible for keeping any passwords or login credentials confidential.

8.3 Security Breaches

  • If a security breach occurs that compromises your personal information, we will notify you and the Data Protection Commission as soon as reasonably possible, as required by law.

9. Data Retention

9.1 How Long We Keep Data

  • Client Records: Kept for as long as legally required for tax and accounting purposes (typically 5-7 years) plus a reasonable period afterward to handle post-engagement questions.
  • Inquiries: Form submissions that do not lead to an engagement are purged on a rolling basis.
  • Marketing Data: Kept until you unsubscribe or request deletion.

9.2 Deletion

  • When we no longer need your data, we will securely delete or anonymize it in a way that prevents reconstruction.
